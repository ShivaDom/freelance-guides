How to Email a Professor: Structure and Examples

Professors get dozens of emails a day. Make yours easy to answer.

Structure

  1. Subject: specific, e.g. "Question about Assignment 2, Section 3".
  2. Greeting: "Dear Professor [Surname],".
  3. Who you are: name, course and section in one line.
  4. The request: one clear question or ask.
  5. What you already tried: shows effort.
  6. Thanks and sign-off with your full name.

Example

Dear Professor Lee, I am Sara Ahmed from your Tuesday seminar. I could not find the submission deadline for the second essay on the syllabus or the course page. Could you confirm it? Thank you for your time. Best regards, Sara Ahmed.
