Freelance Contract Basics: 8 Things to Put in Writing

A clear agreement prevents most disputes. This is general information, not legal advice.

  • Scope and deliverables
  • Price and payment schedule
  • Deposit amount
  • Number of revisions
  • Deadlines and client responsibilities
  • Ownership of the finished work
  • Late payment terms
  • How either side can end the agreement

Keep it to one or two pages. Both sides should confirm it in writing before work starts.
