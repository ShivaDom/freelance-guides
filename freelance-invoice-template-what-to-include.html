What to Include on a Freelance Invoice

  • The word "Invoice" and a unique invoice number
  • Issue date and due date
  • Your name and contact details, and the client's
  • A clear description of each item or service
  • Quantity, rate and total per line
  • Taxes if they apply to you
  • Payment methods and details
  • Late payment terms you agreed in advance

Send the invoice the day you deliver, and state the due date in the email too.
