7 Freelance Pricing Mistakes to Avoid

  • Pricing by hour only: faster work earns you less.
  • No deposit: it protects you from non-payment and filters out unserious clients.
  • Unlimited revisions: state a number in writing.
  • Quoting before understanding scope: ask questions first.
  • Discounting without removing scope.
  • Forgetting taxes and downtime in your rate.
  • Never raising prices.
