How to Ask for a Deadline Extension Politely

Ask as soon as you know you will be late, not on the deadline day.

What to include

  • The task and original deadline
  • A short, honest reason (no long story)
  • A specific new date you can meet
  • What you have already completed

Example

Hello [Name], I want to flag early that I need more time on [task], due [date]. [One-sentence reason.] I can deliver the complete version by [new date], and I can send the finished parts by [date] so you can review them meanwhile. Would that work?
