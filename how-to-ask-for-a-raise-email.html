How to Ask for a Raise by Email

Prepare

List your achievements with measurable results, check typical pay for your role and location, and decide your target figure and your minimum.

Email outline

  1. Ask for a short meeting to discuss compensation.
  2. In the meeting, present results first, then your request.
  3. Confirm the outcome in writing afterwards.

Meeting request example

Hi [Name], I would like to schedule 20 minutes to discuss my role and compensation. Over the past year I [result 1] and [result 2]. Could we find a time this week or next?
