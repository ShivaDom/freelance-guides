How to Ask a Client for a Testimonial

The best time to ask is right after delivery, when the client is happy.

Make it easy

Send two or three short questions instead of asking for a free-form review: what problem did they have, what did you do, what changed.

Ask permission

Confirm they are happy for their name and company to be shown.

Follow up once

If there is no reply in a week, send one polite reminder and then let it go.
