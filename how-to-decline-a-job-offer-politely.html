How to Decline a Job Offer Politely

Decline quickly, in writing, and be grateful.

Four parts

  1. Thank them for the offer and their time.
  2. State clearly that you are declining.
  3. Give a brief reason if you wish (not required).
  4. Wish them well and offer to stay in touch.

Example

Dear [Name], thank you for offering me the [role] position and for the time your team spent with me. After careful thought, I have decided to decline. I was impressed by the team and wish you every success in finding the right person. I hope our paths cross again.
