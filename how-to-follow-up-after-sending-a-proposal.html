How to Follow Up After Sending a Proposal

Silence after a proposal usually means busy, not no.

After 3 days

Ask if they have questions and offer a short call.

After 7 days

Add something useful: a relevant example or a small idea for their project.

After 14 days

Send a closing note saying you will assume timing has changed, and that they are welcome to reach out later.
