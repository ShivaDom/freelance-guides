How to Follow Up on a Job Application

Wait one to two weeks after applying, unless the posting gives a date.

Example

Dear [Name], I applied for the [role] on [date] and remain very interested. I believe my experience in [skill] fits your needs. Could you tell me where the process stands? Thank you for your time.

Send one follow-up, then a final short note after another week.
