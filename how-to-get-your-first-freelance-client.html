How to Get Your First Freelance Client

The first client usually comes from people who already know you, not from job boards.

Week 1: Define one offer

Write one sentence: who you help, with what, and the result. A narrow offer is easier to sell than "I do everything".

Week 2: Tell your network

Message 20 people directly with your offer and ask if they know someone who needs it.

Week 3: Send targeted pitches

Pick 15 businesses, mention one specific thing about each, and offer a small first project.

Week 4: Follow up

Most replies come from the second or third message.
