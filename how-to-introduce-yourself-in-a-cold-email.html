How to Introduce Yourself in a Cold Email

Your introduction should take one sentence. Spend the rest of the email on the reader's situation.

Example

Hi [Name], I am [Name], a [role] who helps [type of business] with [problem]. I noticed [specific observation about them]. I recently helped [similar client] achieve [result]. Would a short call next week be useful?

Follow up twice, a few days apart, with something new each time.
