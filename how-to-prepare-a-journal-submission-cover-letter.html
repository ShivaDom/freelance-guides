How to Write a Cover Letter for a Journal Submission

The cover letter is read by the editor before the reviewers see anything.

Include

  • Title and article type
  • Two or three sentences on the contribution
  • Why it fits this journal
  • Statements on originality, no duplicate submission, and conflicts of interest
  • Corresponding author details

Check the journal's own instructions and follow them exactly. See The Researcher's AI Prompt Pack for prompts that help with drafting and editing.
