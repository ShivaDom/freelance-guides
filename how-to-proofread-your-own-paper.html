How to Proofread Your Own Paper

  • Leave it for a day, then read it again.
  • Read aloud or have it read to you.
  • Check one thing at a time: numbers, references, then grammar.
  • Read backwards, sentence by sentence, for typos.
  • Print it, or change the font, to see it fresh.

Tools can point out issues, but you remain responsible for every sentence and reference.
