How to Respond to Reviewer Comments on a Paper

Reviewers are volunteers. A clear response letter raises your chances.

Format

Quote each comment, then give your response and the exact change (with page or line). Thank the reviewer once at the top.

When you disagree

Explain your reasoning with evidence and, where reasonable, add a sentence to the paper that addresses the concern.
