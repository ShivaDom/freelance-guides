How to Send a Payment Reminder Email

First reminder

Hi [Name], a quick reminder that invoice [number] for [amount] was due on [date]. I have attached a copy. Please let me know if you need anything from me to process it.

Second reminder

Hi [Name], invoice [number] is now [X] days overdue. Please arrange payment by [date]. If there is a problem, tell me so we can solve it.

Always include the invoice number, amount, due date and payment details.
