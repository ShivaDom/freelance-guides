How to Set Your Freelance Rates

Rates set by guesswork lead to burnout or lost jobs. Start with numbers.

1. Find your floor

Add monthly expenses, taxes and savings, then divide by the billable hours you can realistically work (usually 50-60% of your week).

2. Check the market

Compare three to five freelancers with similar skills and experience.

3. Price on value

If your work earns or saves the client money, charge a share of that result rather than hours.

4. Raise rates on schedule

Raise prices for new clients every few months, and give existing clients notice in writing.
