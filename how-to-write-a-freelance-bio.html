How to Write a Freelance Bio That Gets Replies

A good bio is about the client, not you.

Formula

  1. Who you help.
  2. What problem you solve.
  3. One proof point (result, years, notable client).
  4. How to start (one clear next step).

Avoid vague words like "passionate" and "dynamic". Use concrete nouns and numbers you can back up.
