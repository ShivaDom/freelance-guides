How to Structure a Literature Review (With Careful AI Help)

A review is an argument about what is known and what is missing, not a list of summaries.

Steps

  1. Define the research question.
  2. Collect sources and note claim, method and limits for each.
  3. Group sources by theme.
  4. Write each section around a point, with sources as evidence.
  5. End with the gap your study fills.

Where AI can help

Ask it to suggest theme groupings of notes you wrote, or to point out unclear transitions. Verify every source yourself and follow your institution's AI rules.
