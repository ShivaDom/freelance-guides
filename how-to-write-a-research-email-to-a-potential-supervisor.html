How to Email a Potential PhD Supervisor

  1. Specific subject line: "Prospective PhD student: [topic]".
  2. One sentence on who you are and your degree.
  3. Name one or two of their papers and say what interests you.
  4. Outline your research idea in three sentences.
  5. Ask if they are accepting students and attach your CV.

Keep it under 200 words and personalise every email.
