How to Write a Scope of Work

  1. Objective: what the project achieves.
  2. Deliverables: exact items, formats, quantities.
  3. Out of scope: list common extras you are not doing.
  4. Timeline: milestones with dates.
  5. Client inputs: what you need from them and by when.
  6. Change process: extra work is quoted separately.
