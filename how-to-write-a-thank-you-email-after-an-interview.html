How to Write a Thank-You Email After an Interview

Include

  • Thanks for their time
  • One specific thing you discussed that you found interesting
  • One sentence on why you are a fit
  • A clear close

Example

Dear [Name], thank you for speaking with me today about the [role]. I enjoyed hearing about [specific topic]. It strengthened my interest, and I believe my experience in [skill] would help with [their goal]. Please let me know if I can provide anything further. Best regards, [Name].

Keep it under 120 words and proofread it before sending.
