Out-of-Office Message Examples for Email

Formula

Say you are away, give the return date, say when they can expect a reply, and give an alternative contact for urgent matters.

Example

Hello, and thank you for your message. I am out of the office from [date] to [date] with limited access to email. I will reply when I return on [date]. For urgent matters, please contact [Name] at [email].
